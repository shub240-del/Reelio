# RuffCut Website - Design Brainstorm

This is a direct replication of the RuffCut website (useruffcut.com). The design must match the reference exactly.

## Reference Approach (Chosen)

The design follows the actual RuffCut website aesthetic faithfully.

**Design Movement**: Dark modern SaaS landing page with high-contrast typography and orange accent system.

**Core Principles**:
1. Very dark background (#0a0a0f) creates depth and focus
2. Orange (#f97316) as the signature accent for CTAs, badges, and highlights
3. Bold, large display typography for impact headings
4. Card-based content organization with subtle borders

**Color Philosophy**:
- Background: near-black (#0a0a0f)
- Card backgrounds: slightly elevated dark (#141420)
- Orange accent: #f97316
- White for primary text, muted gray for secondary
- Subtle blue in the CTA banner section

**Layout Paradigm**: Full-width sections with contained content, card grids for features, horizontal step flow for process, stacked card carousel for use cases.

**Signature Elements**:
1. Orange numbered badges on tool cards
2. Waveform-style visualizations in feature cards
3. Stacked/overlapping card carousel for content types

**Typography System**: Inter or similar sans-serif, bold weights for headings (48-64px), regular for body

**Brand Essence**: AI-powered video editor that's free, simple, and playful — "Your dawg that edits for you."

**Brand Voice**: Direct, confident, slightly playful. "No more boring, repetitive editing." "Free to use editor. No credit card required."

**Logo**: RuffCut text logo with a small dog mascot icon

**Signature Brand Color**: Orange (#f97316)
